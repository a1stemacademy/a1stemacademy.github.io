Key Components of High School English Training:

General English (High School Level):

  • Literary Analysis: Understanding and analyzing key themes, characters, and literary techniques in classic and modern texts.
  • Writing Skills: Crafting clear, effective essays, improving sentence structure, and enhancing vocabulary.
  • Reading Comprehension: Developing the ability to interpret and analyze different types of texts, including fiction, nonfiction, poetry, and drama.
  • Grammar and Syntax: Building a solid foundation in the rules of grammar and the structure of written language.
  • Creative Writing: Developing storytelling skills through narrative, poetry, and descriptive writing.
  • Training Approach:
    • Focus on developing well-rounded literacy skills that include writing, reading, listening, and speaking.
    • Interactive activities to make reading enjoyable and insightful, encouraging students to connect personally with the material.
    • Emphasis on practical applications of English for school assignments, essays, and other assessments.


Honors English (Advanced High School Level):

  • Advanced Literary Analysis: In-depth analysis of classic and contemporary literature, including novels, plays, and poetry.
  • Critical Thinking and Argumentation: Developing the ability to engage in critical discussions, form persuasive arguments, and write essays with strong, evidence-backed claims.
  • Essay Writing and Research: Mastering research papers, argumentative essays, and literary criticism with a focus on analytical writing.
  • Poetry and Drama: Analyzing and interpreting various forms of poetry, drama, and other literary genres with an advanced focus on symbolism, motifs, and literary devices.
  • Creative Writing: Encouraging originality and creativity in writing, exploring diverse genres such as short stories, poetry, and essays.
  • Training Approach:
    • Advanced reading assignments and discussions that challenge students to think critically and engage in nuanced interpretations.
    • Practice writing assignments that encourage the development of clear, coherent arguments with well-researched evidence.
    • Emphasis on analytical writing and creativity, with personalized feedback to improve individual writing style and voice.
    • Detailed study of literary theories and critical perspectives to develop a well-rounded understanding of literature.


AP English Literature & Composition (AP Lit):

  • Literary Analysis and Interpretation: Detailed analysis of classic literature, including novels, plays, and poetry from various periods (e.g., Romantic, Modern, and Contemporary).
  • Character and Theme Analysis: Deep exploration of character development, themes, motifs, and symbols in major literary works.
  • Literary Devices: Mastery of literary terms and devices such as symbolism, metaphor, irony, and allegory, and understanding how they function within texts.
  • Essay Writing: Crafting detailed analytical essays, including comparative analysis, argument essays, and interpretive writing that demonstrates a mastery of language and argumentation.
  • Exam Preparation: Preparing students for the AP English Literature exam through practice questions, timed writing exercises, and deep text analysis.
  • Training Approach:
    • Extensive practice with AP exam-style essays and free-response questions to build confidence in writing under timed conditions.
    • Encouragement of original analysis and thought, with a focus on the deeper meanings behind texts.
    • Mock AP exams and focused exam prep sessions to hone writing skills and test-taking strategies.
    • Emphasis on deep reading and critical analysis of diverse literary works to prepare for the AP exam's focus on analysis and interpretation.


AP English Language & Composition (AP Lang):

  • Rhetorical Analysis: Understanding and analyzing the rhetorical strategies used in various texts (essays, speeches, advertisements, etc.).
  • Argumentative Writing: Developing the skills to create persuasive arguments, support claims with evidence, and use rhetoric effectively.
  • Synthesis Writing: Writing essays that synthesize multiple sources, analyzing different viewpoints on a given issue.
  • Nonfiction Texts: Analyzing a wide range of nonfiction texts, including essays, speeches, and articles, with a focus on rhetoric, tone, and audience.
  • Exam Preparation: Preparing for the AP English Language exam with practice essays, timed responses, and a deep understanding of rhetorical analysis.
  • Training Approach:
    • Focus on building strong argumentative writing skills with emphasis on structure, clarity, and persuasive techniques.
    • Use of real-world texts (speeches, advertisements, essays) to analyze the effective use of rhetoric and language.
    • Regular practice with AP-style multiple-choice questions and timed writing tasks to ensure readiness for the exam.
    • Personalized feedback on essays, helping students refine their writing style and develop effective rhetorical strategies.
