Key Components of High School Physics Training:

AP Physics 1:

  • Kinematics: Motion in one and two dimensions, displacement, velocity, and acceleration.
  • Newton’s Laws of Motion: Forces and motion, equilibrium, and dynamics.
  • Work, Energy, and Power: Concepts of mechanical work, energy conservation, and power.
  • Momentum and Impulse: Collisions, impulse-momentum theorem, and conservation of momentum.
  • Circular Motion and Gravitation: Dynamics of objects moving in circles and universal law of gravitation.
  • Simple Harmonic Motion: Oscillations, springs, pendulums, and wave motion.
  • Training Approach:
    • Focus on both conceptual understanding and problem-solving skills.
    • Interactive labs and practical applications to reinforce theoretical concepts.
    • Mock exams and practice questions to prepare for the AP exam format.


AP Physics 2:

  • Fluid Mechanics: Fluid statics, dynamics, and applications (Bernoulli's equation, fluid flow).
  • Thermodynamics: Heat, temperature, first and second laws of thermodynamics, and engines.
  • Electricity and Magnetism: Electrostatics, circuits, magnetic fields, and electromagnetic induction.
  • Optics: Reflection, refraction, and wave properties of light; interference, diffraction, and polarization.
  • Modern Physics: Quantum mechanics, atomic models, nuclear physics, and special relativity.
  • Training Approach:
    • In-depth exploration of key principles, providing a comprehensive understanding of intermediate-level physics.
    • Use of simulations and hands-on labs to make abstract concepts tangible.
    • Focused review sessions to tackle complex problems, preparing students for high-level AP exam questions.


AP Physics C (Mechanics and Electricity & Magnetism):

  • AP Physics C: Mechanics:
    • Kinematics: Advanced motion in multiple dimensions, free fall, and projectile motion.
    • Newton’s Laws: Applications in friction, tension, and forces in equilibrium.
    • Work, Energy, and Power: Detailed work-energy theorem and energy conservation in complex systems.
    • Rotational Motion: Moment of inertia, torque, angular momentum, and rotational dynamics.
    • Gravitation: Universal gravitation and orbital mechanics.
  • AP Physics C: Electricity & Magnetism:
    • Electrostatics: Electric fields, Coulomb’s Law, electric potential, and capacitors.
    • Electric Circuits: Kirchhoff’s laws, resistors, capacitors, and RC circuits.
    • Magnetic Fields and Forces: Magnetic force on moving charges, Ampère’s Law, and electromagnetic induction.
    • Maxwell’s Equations: Foundations of electromagnetism and wave propagation.
  • Training Approach:
    • Rigorous problem-solving techniques, with a focus on calculus-based physics.
    • Interactive learning with mathematical derivations and in-depth theoretical explanations.
    • Extensive practice with AP-level problem sets to prepare students for the highly demanding exam.
